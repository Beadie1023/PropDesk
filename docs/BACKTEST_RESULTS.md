# Backtest results: Lorentzian signals on GBP/AUD 30-minute data

Generated by `npx tsx scripts/backtest.ts <csv>` from `time_series-GBP_AUD-30min.csv`
(5000 candles, 2026-07-10 to 2026-10-22). R = multiples of the amount risked per trade.

## Read this first

- **Only about 3 months and 60 to 140 trades.** That is too little to prove an edge, and it covers one market regime (a range).
- **The data has quality problems.** It contains Saturday and Sunday candles with real price movement, which GBP/AUD does not trade, and it does not line up with the TradingView (OANDA) candles in your screenshots. The first section removes the weekend closure; the second uses the file as exported. Both reach the same conclusion. Re-export from the feed you trade before trusting any number here.
- **This tests PropDesk's re-implementation, not the TradingView script.** It does not reproduce the 60.7% win rate TradingView shows on your chart (see the fixed-hold table), so the two may differ in ways that cannot be checked from here.
- **Not tested:** the Probability Gate's daily 50-day-average filter, structural stops and zones. They need far more than 100 days of data.
- Rules were fixed before looking at results: stop 1.5 x ATR, target 1:2, 3-pip round-trip cost, one trade at a time, entry at the next bar's open, stop assumed first if both are inside one bar.
